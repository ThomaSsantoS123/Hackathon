
Story arc
1. How humans built trust

Trust was memory, not score

You knew who helped when your roof leaked

Reciprocity: I help you, you help me

Social: lived in relationships, gossip, shared memory

Slow, but durable

2. Why humans need social

Humans are not self-sufficient

Need each other for childcare, food, shelter

Trust is the currency of that infrastructure

Before money → credit. Before credit → trust

Finance built on top of trust, not instead of it

3. How we lost it

Moved to cities, stopped knowing neighbors

Coordinated through apps — but apps have no memory

Help someone move → chat scrolls away

No record, no credit, no trust that travels

Same 3 people do everything, everyone else invisible

4. What we lost

Not survival — we survived fine

Lost the bonus

Neighbor who knew your name

Friend who owed you a favor

Feeling of being useful

Life got thinner

5. The financial cost

Trust doesn't travel → value gets stranded

Retired plumber's skills unused

Parent's cooking unrecognized

Tutor's time wasted on marketing

Informal economy has no ledger

Can't scale, can't be measured, can't be financed

6. The insight

Trust isn't a score

Trust is a path

You trust someone who trusts someone who trusts them

7. The rebuild

Trust coordination layer

Every member vouched in

Vouch carries liability

1 hour = 1 credit

App shows the path, not strangers

Mei → Alice → Ben → Sam

8. A bonus layer

Not required for survival

But much better to have

Credits are a bonus

Connection is the point

People need to be needed
